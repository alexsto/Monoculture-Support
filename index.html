Frequently asked questions

Which cameras are supported?
Any camera. Monoculture reads DNG (including iPhone ProRAW), JPEG and HEIC/HEIF files (including Fujifilm and Sony .HIF) from iPhone, Leica, Fujifilm, Sony, Hasselblad, Nikon, Canon and others. Leica Q owners also get the in-camera digital frame lines in Crop. Proprietary raw formats (RAF, ARW, CR3, NEF, 3FR) aren't read directly — convert them to DNG with Adobe DNG Converter first.
How do I import photos?
Choose Import and pick your card, a folder or individual files. A raw file and the JPEG or HEIC of the same frame are kept together, with the DNG as the master. Your originals are copied into the app and never changed.
Can I create or share my own looks?
Yes. Save any edit as a look, or import and export looks as small JSON files from the ⋯ menu above the Looks strip.
Will my edits open in Lightroom or Photoshop?
Export as DNG with “Raw + editable settings” to keep your original raw data and open your edits as Camera Raw settings. Dodge & burn masks are the only edits that don't carry over.
Does Monoculture upload my photos or need an account?
No. Everything happens on your device. There is no cloud storage, no account, no tracking and no subscription.
