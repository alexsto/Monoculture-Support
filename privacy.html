Monoculture

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Monoculture: B&W Photo Editor for macOS, iOS and iPadOS · Future Pixels Studio LLC

The short version. Monoculture does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Your photos never leave your device. The app uses no cloud storage, no online services, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it requires no account or subscription.

What the app does with your photos

  • Photos you import (DNG, JPEG and HEIC files) are copied into Monoculture's own library on your device, inside the app's private storage. The original files on your card or in your folders are never changed.
  • Your edits (layers, looks, history and snapshots) are saved alongside the library on your device.
  • Custom looks you import or create are saved on your device.
  • Exports (JPEG, PNG or DNG) are written only to the location you choose.
  • All processing — black-and-white conversion, looks, crop, export — happens entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, Monoculture does not collect:

  • personal information such as your name, email address or contact details
  • your photos, their contents, or their metadata (such as camera, lens, date or location)
  • usage data, analytics, diagnostics or crash reports
  • device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • location data

Monoculture contains no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries or advertising frameworks, and it does not connect to any server operated by us or by anyone else.

Photo metadata

Your photos may contain metadata recorded by your camera, including GPS location. Monoculture reads this metadata only on your device, to show capture details and to keep it in exports when you choose to. The export options include Strip GPS, which removes location data from exported JPEG and PNG files. This metadata is never sent anywhere by the app.

Device backups and Apple services

Monoculture's library lives in the app's private storage on your device. If you use your device's own backup features (such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup), that storage may be included in those backups under Apple's terms, which you control in your device settings. Monoculture itself does not upload anything. If you share or save exported files using the system share sheet or file dialogs, they go to the destination you choose, under that service's terms.

Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics (such as download counts) and, only if you have opted in to share them with developers, crash reports. These are collected by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by Monoculture.

Children

Monoculture does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You can delete your Monoculture library at any time by deleting photos in the app or by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Any change that would introduce data collection would be described here before it takes effect, and would require your consent where the law requires it.
